# Froganize 官网故事插图记录

四张故事图采用已确认的透明蛙仔立绘作为**角色身份参考**，通过参考图引导的
图像生成完成。参考源是：

```text
../../media/brand/froganize-mascot-transparent.png
```

输出先生成 PNG，再以 JPEG 品质 84 转换为官网素材。页面没有使用生成图中的
文字来表达产品规则；所有准确文案均由 HTML 呈现。

V3 中，这四幅图作为一组连续绘本场景，通过页面底色、渐变蒙版和充足留白与
背景融为一体。它们不作为四张独立照片卡片，也不通过额外的“听故事”
按钮才能进入；用户在正常浏览产品页时会自然经过完整四幕。

## 共同身份锚点

> The attached transparent Froganize mascot is the exact character identity
> reference. Preserve the butter-yellow nearly spherical trapezoid frog,
> broad front-facing symmetry, cobalt circular eye plates, flat coral
> horizontal pupils with absolutely no reflections, tiny blue nostrils,
> minimal blue mouth, slightly extended little frog legs, soft premium
> 3D-clay finish, and restrained warm-cream/cobalt/coral palette. The frog is
> not green, not humanoid, and must remain the same character. Use a wide 3:2
> editorial website composition with quiet cream studio lighting, rounded
> forms, generous breathing room, and a sophisticated productivity-app visual
> language. No logos, watermark, border, extra character, photorealism, green
> frog, human anatomy, or overly busy background.

## `story-01-chaos.jpg`

> Before learning to organize, Froganize sits at a calm cream desktop, mildly
> confused by a small tasteful mess of cobalt folders, paper sheets, and two
> unlabeled files. The mess is readable but not stressful. It does not yet
> have a magic wand. Keep the character centered enough for responsive crop.

## `story-02-wand.jpg`

> Froganize has just discovered a magical organizing wand beside a few mildly
> scattered folders. The wand is a slender natural warm-brown wooden staff
> with a solid cobalt folder-shaped magical head physically attached at one
> end. Froganize leans toward it with gentle surprise. A restrained golden glow
> surrounds only the folder-shaped tip. Do not turn it into a star, broom,
> sword, ordinary stick, or loose folder.

## `story-03-learning.jpg`

> Froganize is learning the organizing magic while holding the warm-brown
> wooden wand with its cobalt folder-shaped head clearly visible. Three or four
> cobalt folders float in one controlled arc from a small desktop pile toward
> simple year-and-month cubbies. Keep the process calm, focused, and easy to
> understand, with only subtle golden particles.

## `story-04-master.jpg`

> Froganize has mastered organizing and stands calmly beside ordered cobalt
> year-and-month folder cubbies, holding its familiar cobalt folder. The wooden
> staff with attached cobalt folder-shaped head rests visibly at its side. The
> desktop is spacious and clean, and the final mood is serene, capable, safe,
> and reversible rather than theatrical.
