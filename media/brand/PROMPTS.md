# Froganize illustration prompts

The three campaign illustrations were generated in **reference-guided image
editing mode** from the approved transparent mascot. This file records the
accepted creative briefs so future additions preserve the same identity.

## Shared identity anchor

> Use the referenced Froganize mascot as the exact identity anchor. Preserve
> the butter-yellow, nearly spherical trapezoid frog; broad front-facing
> symmetry; cobalt circular eye plates; flat coral horizontal pupils with no
> reflections; tiny blue nostrils and minimal blue mouth; slightly extended
> little legs; and the cobalt folder held at the center. Keep the soft premium
> 3D-clay finish, warm cream, cobalt, and restrained coral palette. Do not make
> the frog green or humanoid. No text, letters, logos, watermark, border, or
> extra character.

## `froganize-welcome.png`

> Create a wide 3:2 editorial welcome scene using the shared identity anchor.
> Place Froganize on the right in a calm welcoming pose inside a clean,
> softly-lit cream studio. Leave generous uncluttered negative space on the
> left for later typography. Add only subtle cobalt architectural curves and
> warm shadows. The frog and central folder remain the clear subject.

## `froganize-organizing.png`

> Create a wide 3:2 working scene using the shared identity anchor. Froganize
> gently organizes a few cobalt folders from one mildly cluttered desktop tray
> into tidy year-and-month cubbies. Make the Before-to-Timeline flow readable
> without arrows or text. Keep the scene sparse, reassuring, symmetrical where
> practical, and clearly about careful organization rather than deletion.

## `froganize-portrait.png`

> Create a premium square character portrait using the shared identity anchor.
> Show only the complete Froganize mascot as the dominant centered subject,
> holding its cobalt folder. Use a quiet warm-cream field with a restrained
> cobalt halo and very soft grounding shadow. No room, desk, extra objects, or
> additional characters.

Exact product copy, diagrams, and social layouts are added later by
`scripts/build_media.py`; generated image text is never used.
